# Original art for ОТ СТАРОТО · revision 3

The supplied references guide the bold black ink, expressive rubber-hose faces, worn Bulgarian street setting and restrained scarlet/amber palette. These are new illustrations. The reference images, their lettering and commercial slot assets are not shipped.

`symbols-ink.png` is an original, transparent 1254×1254 atlas containing nine distinct paying objects: bottle, cash, chain, cassette, sneaker, crown, lighter, dice and signet ring. Its thick black outlines, flat ivory fills, charcoal shadows and small red/mustard accents match the character drawings. This replaces revision 2's glossy glass and engraved metal objects. Every symbol has a precise independent display window; the atlas pixels remain intact.

Each character now has **eight separately illustrated full-body key drawings**, split between two transparent 1536×1024 sheets: `character-left-motion-a.png` / `character-left-motion-b.png`, `character-middle-motion-a.png` / `character-middle-motion-b.png`, and `character-right-motion-a.png` / `character-right-motion-b.png`. Each sheet holds four poses with enough space for the complete head, shoes, hands and action props. The eight drawings follow idle, anticipation, preparation, wind-up/aim, release, follow-through/recoil, recovery and settle. They preserve the fictional sunglasses/Wild thrower, fringe-haired shooter and lean gold-toothed coin dealer. These are new articulated drawings with changes in limbs, expression and body weight; they are not eight transformed copies of one picture.

`getaway-car.png` is an original transparent 1536×1024 detailed illustration of a battered fictional Lada-style sedan with three adult characters and muzzle flashes. `ruse-yard.png` is the original 1672×941 fictional Ruse courtyard from revision 1; its architecture and evening lighting remain the scene background.

The revision 3 symbol and character sheets were produced with image generation on 9 October 2026 (Europe/Sofia), using the earlier original characters as visual references. The yard and car were generated on 8 October. These are generated illustrations, not photographs or a claim of traditional hand-drawn production. The Wild star, Bonus ticket, MAX target and four ink coin plates are separately authored SVG drawings. The MAX plate is a grid symbol with a clear 19,999× badge; coin SVGs reserve space for the live receipt values drawn by the renderer.

`src/art.ts` supplies precise display windows and the eight-frame sequence. Each frame records a common body width/height, its own foot baseline and a stable horizontal body anchor. The release drawing also records the palm or revolver-barrel attachment point so a projectile starts at the character's hand. The board and win-scene Canvas renderers use the same raw atlas URLs and source rectangles; they decode six character atlases once rather than warming 24 full-atlas SVG copies. The legacy cropped URL helpers remain lazy for the three idle buy-card illustrations. In-between motion, recoil, target flights and effects are rendered from the immutable round receipt.

[The actual Chromium contact sheet](../../docs/art/animation-contact-sheet.png) shows all 24 source-window character frames and all nine regular symbols. [The source-hashed validation record](../../docs/art/art-validation.json) records successful decodes, body/foot/release anchors and alpha-component checks: every principal drawing is complete inside its display window and no adjacent actor or object appears inside another window.

Revision 1 drawings are archived in `docs/archive-art-v1/`. Revision 2's original sheets and glossy object atlas remain in [the published revision 2 commit](https://github.com/stoyanov808/Test/tree/99c4482db6b83a52072d302740df05ed1fa13f8a/variants/ot-staroto/public/art); unused versions are not copied into the active game build. No web photographs, commercial slot sprites or recordings are included. Audio replacement is configured through repository files and code; the game has no custom-audio upload interface.
