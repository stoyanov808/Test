# Original art for ОТ СТАРОТО · revision 5

The supplied references guide the bold black ink, expressive rubber-hose faces and restrained scarlet/amber palette. These are new illustrations. The reference images, their lettering and commercial slot assets are not shipped.

`symbols-ink.png` is an original, transparent 1254×1254 atlas containing nine distinct paying objects: bottle, cash, chain, cassette, sneaker, crown, lighter, dice and signet ring. Its thick black outlines, flat ivory fills, charcoal shadows and small red/mustard accents match the character drawings. This replaces revision 2's glossy glass and engraved metal objects. Every symbol has a precise independent display window; the atlas pixels remain intact.

Each character has **16 separately illustrated action drawings**. The left Wild thrower and middle Shooter interleave their eight original drawings with eight newly illustrated in-between poses in `character-left-motion-c.png` / `-d.png` and `character-middle-motion-c.png` / `-d.png`. LUX uses 16 entirely new seated drawings in `character-right-seated-a.png` through `-d.png`; standing drawings are not mixed into his seated sequence. Each transparent sheet contains four independently posed figures. The old right preparation sheet supplies only the unchanged paying-symbol portrait.

LUX is a separate animated sprite sitting on the **speaker already drawn on the right of the club background**. His per-pose pelvis anchor stays on its top during lean, reveal and recovery. On narrow screens where centered cover crops that speaker out of view, the renderer draws a source window of the same original speaker under his seat. It does not introduce a different speaker or bake the character into the background. The larger foreground actors are drawn after the board without its clipping mask; the intentional expanded Shooter reel avatars remain inside their own columns.

`student-club-ink.png` is the original 1672×941 student-party club interior used as the scene background. Cream arches, a red booth, a record poster, a bottle bar, loudspeakers and parquet floor use the same flat ivory/black drawing language as the objects and characters. The central wall and floor are deliberately quiet so the reels stay readable; the scenery frames the edges of the full game window. This replaces the Ruse courtyard. There is no embedded lettering, photographed building or commercial game artwork.

`getaway-car.png` is an original transparent 1536×1024 detailed illustration of a battered fictional Lada-style sedan with three adult characters and muzzle flashes. The fictional Ruse courtyard remains only in the revision 1 archive and published revision 3 history; it is not copied into the active game build.

The revision 3 symbol and character sheets were produced with image generation on 9 October 2026 (Europe/Sofia), using the earlier original characters as visual references. The revision 4 club interior was produced with image generation on the same date, using the original ink object atlas and left-character sheet as palette and linework references. The nine paying objects and 16 original left/middle actor drawings remain unchanged. Revision 5 adds eight new in-between drawings for each and 16 new seated LUX drawings on 10 October 2026, using the existing character sheets as identity references. Generation and spacing corrections used the image generation tool; no raster-edit script altered the resulting illustrations. The archived yard and active car were generated on 8 October. These are generated illustrations, not photographs or a claim of traditional hand-drawn production. The Wild star, Bonus ticket, MAX target and four ink coin plates are separately authored SVG drawings. The MAX plate is a grid symbol with a clear 19,999× badge; coin SVGs reserve space for the live receipt values drawn by the renderer.

`src/art.ts` and `src/character-frames.json` supply source windows, 16-frame order, body dimensions, foot anchors, seated pelvis anchors and release attachments. The release occurs at frame 8. The board and win-scene renderers share raw atlases and animate anticipation, release, follow-through and recovery. Per-frame body reference heights normalize drawings of different source sizes, while a stable full-pose envelope prevents gesture-dependent scaling. Body anchors keep gestures from shifting the actor's placement; release attachments start projectiles at the illustrated hand or barrel. The source pixels remain intact, including very faint alpha halos inherited from the original style.

The unchanged background retains its separately dated revision 4 [landscape/portrait cover evidence](../../docs/art/scene-cover-contact-sheet.png). Historical archived artwork is not loaded by the game. No web photographs, commercial slot sprites or recordings are included. Audio replacement is configured through repository files and code; the game has no custom-audio upload interface.
