# Original art for ОТ СТАРОТО · revision 4

The supplied references guide the bold black ink, expressive rubber-hose faces and restrained scarlet/amber palette. These are new illustrations. The reference images, their lettering and commercial slot assets are not shipped.

`symbols-ink.png` is an original, transparent 1254×1254 atlas containing nine distinct paying objects: bottle, cash, chain, cassette, sneaker, crown, lighter, dice and signet ring. Its thick black outlines, flat ivory fills, charcoal shadows and small red/mustard accents match the character drawings. This replaces revision 2's glossy glass and engraved metal objects. Every symbol has a precise independent display window; the atlas pixels remain intact.

Each character now has **eight separately illustrated full-body key drawings**, split between two transparent 1536×1024 sheets: `character-left-motion-a.png` / `character-left-motion-b.png`, `character-middle-motion-a.png` / `character-middle-motion-b.png`, and `character-right-motion-a.png` / `character-right-motion-b.png`. Each sheet holds four poses with enough space for the complete head, shoes, hands and action props. The eight drawings follow idle, anticipation, preparation, wind-up/aim, release, follow-through/recoil, recovery and settle. They preserve the fictional sunglasses/Wild thrower, fringe-haired shooter and lean gold-toothed coin dealer. These are new articulated drawings with changes in limbs, expression and body weight; they are not eight transformed copies of one picture.

`student-club-ink.png` is the original 1672×941 student-party club interior used as the scene background. Cream arches, a red booth, a record poster, a bottle bar, loudspeakers and parquet floor use the same flat ivory/black drawing language as the objects and characters. The central wall and floor are deliberately quiet so the reels stay readable; the scenery frames the edges of the full game window. This replaces the Ruse courtyard. There is no embedded lettering, photographed building or commercial game artwork.

`getaway-car.png` is an original transparent 1536×1024 detailed illustration of a battered fictional Lada-style sedan with three adult characters and muzzle flashes. The fictional Ruse courtyard remains only in the revision 1 archive and published revision 3 history; it is not copied into the active game build.

The revision 3 symbol and character sheets were produced with image generation on 9 October 2026 (Europe/Sofia), using the earlier original characters as visual references. The revision 4 club interior was produced with image generation on the same date, using the original ink object atlas and left-character sheet as palette and linework references. Those nine paying objects and all 24 actor drawings remain unchanged. The archived yard and active car were generated on 8 October. These are generated illustrations, not photographs or a claim of traditional hand-drawn production. The Wild star, Bonus ticket, MAX target and four ink coin plates are separately authored SVG drawings. The MAX plate is a grid symbol with a clear 19,999× badge; coin SVGs reserve space for the live receipt values drawn by the renderer.

`src/art.ts` supplies precise display windows and the eight-frame sequence. Each frame records a common body width/height, its own foot baseline and a stable horizontal body anchor. The release drawing also records the palm or revolver-barrel attachment point so a projectile starts at the character's hand. The board and win-scene Canvas renderers use the same raw atlas URLs and source rectangles; they decode six character atlases once rather than warming 24 full-atlas SVG copies. The legacy cropped URL helpers remain lazy for the three idle buy-card illustrations. In-between motion, recoil, target flights and effects are rendered from the immutable round receipt.

[The source-hashed validation record](../../docs/art/art-validation.json) records successful decodes, body/foot/release anchors and alpha-component checks: every principal drawing is complete inside its display window and no adjacent actor or object appears inside another window. Its revision 4 scene evidence separately records a fresh Chromium background decode and [landscape/portrait cover previews](../../docs/art/scene-cover-contact-sheet.png); the unchanged atlas checks retain their original date.
