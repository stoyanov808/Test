# ОТ СТАРОТО: the v4 payline mathematical model

**V4 validation passed:** the user's Le Zeus chart is transcribed as 19 fixed paths and independently checked against the supplied diagrams. All 425,984 complete stake outcomes replayed successfully under the changed line, coin and Shooter rules. The exact proof, 57 engine tests and a separate 200,000-round production-Crypto sample are current. The previous v3 reports are preserved under `docs/research/prototype-v3-*` as historical evidence.

The offered modes have **96.5% theoretical expected return**, measured against the amount actually debited, at every supported stake: €0.10, €0.20, €0.50, €1, €2, €5, €10 and €20. This comes from complete integer-cent outcomes and exact rational weights, rather than assuming a small simulation establishes RTP. It is an independent virtual-credit prototype and has no third-party gambling certification.

RTP describes an expectation over many independent paid rounds. An individual round, a player's session or a finite test can return less than 96%, including zero. The game never adjusts odds using wallet size, losses, history or a target payout balance.

## Outcomes and selection

The expanded v4 [catalogue](../src/math-model.json) contains **53,248 complete round entries** in ten pools. Its builder evaluates every entry at all eight stakes: **425,984 complete stake outcomes**. Entries are compact procedural seeds and verified cent payouts; the actual full cascades, expanding Wild reels, optional shots, guaranteed marked coin waves, collectors, retriggers and bonus upgrades are regenerated by the engine. Awards come from the visible mechanic receipts. There is no added floor award, missing-payout top-up or hidden settlement multiplier.

Each pool gives every entry positive integer ticket weight. Calibration increases the weight of an ensemble whose average falls on the other side of the target expectation. The exact weighted sum then equals the target. The builder requires every individual complete outcome to remain at or below 1% of its conditional pool; the resulting exact largest-entry probability is reported in the regenerated proof. This limits the share carried by one repeat sequence. Pool diversity, effective outcome counts, win/cap probabilities and bonus-tier fractions are recorded in [the proof](mathematics-proof.json).

Production selects tickets from independent `crypto.getRandomValues` uint32 words using integer rejection sampling. Rejected modulo-tail words are discarded; large ticket totals use multiple words. The complete consumed entropy tape is recorded in the round. Reload validates and replays that tape, checks the catalogue index, seed, full mechanic receipt, cent award and ledger chain, and consumes no new random word. An entropy failure or catalogue mismatch throws before credits are debited.

`playFixtureRound` is a deterministic QA factory for engine tests and the development-only browser reset hook. Production uses `playRound` and Web Crypto. The raw `simulateRound` factory is for building and independently checking procedural outcomes.

This is a finite outcome catalogue. Its entries remain repeatable, and it does not offer an unlimited set of possible procedural seeds. The complete catalogue and weights are public so that limitation can be assessed directly.

## Paylines and Wilds

Each fixed path names one row on each of the six reels. A line wins only from the first reel, moving left to right without gaps. The single longest matching run of three, four, five or six cells determines its bracket; shorter overlapping brackets are not added. Different winning lines pay separately. Their physical winning positions are removed only once before the next refill.

The nine regular paytables use the supplied Game Info screenshot's 3/4/5/6 brackets, expressed as base-bet multiples:

| Symbols | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Bottle, cash, lighter | 0.1 | 0.3 | 1 | 3 |
| Chain, cassette | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 5 |
| Sneaker, dice | 1 | 2 | 4 | 10 |
| Crown | 2 | 5 | 10 | 50 |
| Ring | 3 | 10 | 20 | 100 |
| Wild only | — | — | — | 200 |

A regular-symbol line requires at least one actual matching regular symbol. Leading active Wilds substitute for the first natural symbol encountered. A six-Wild line instead pays one explicit 200× Wild award, never nine invented regular awards. Three to five Wilds alone do not create a natural-symbol award. Exhausted sticky Wilds break a run for the remainder of that spin, then rearm on the next free spin.

Visible active Wild multipliers retain the existing global rule: their values sum, with a minimum global factor of one, and apply to every winning line. This includes a six-Wild award. Integer-cent line awards are limited by the remaining whole-round cap. Once that budget is exhausted, later lines do not declare unpaid wins.

## Expanding shooter

The middle badge is a rare expanding Wild. It converts all five positions on its own reel. More than one different reel can expand in the same base spin or free spin. Each expansion carries a visible reel factor selected from 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 50 / 100, with raw procedural weights 6500 / 1800 / 800 / 450 / 250 / 120 / 60 / 20 (sum 10,000). Existing Wild multipliers on that reel are multiplied by the visible factor; new Wilds start at that factor. Existing left-sticky coordinates remain planted and retain their updated multiplier; newly expanded Wilds start transient.

All initially landed Shooters expand before any follow-up shots. Each then has a raw procedural 30% check to fire one to three ordered shots at randomly selected regular symbols or Wilds. Scatter invitations, unresolved character badges and MAX are excluded. A regular target becomes a 1× Wild; hitting an existing Wild doubles its multiplier. A transient shot Wild can join all winning lines in one payout batch, then disappears with the removed symbols. A later shot may select the same target again.

Hitting any position on an intact expanded reel doubles all five Wild multipliers. During a bonus, the entire reel becomes sticky with those exact multipliers until the bonus ends, including later tier upgrades and retriggered spins. Sticky Wilds join one winning batch per free spin, rest for its remaining cascades and rearm on the next free spin. Base-game expanded reels never become bonus-sticky and their Wilds do not carry into a newly triggered bonus. The raw 30% check is not a promise of a 30% paid-play frequency: complete outcome weights also affect the final frequency, reported separately in the proof.

Before the initial drop is recorded, a shooter reel is separated from scatters and other unresolved character badges, and has at most one shooter source. This permits a literal full-reel expansion without erasing an invitation, another feature or an existing sticky identity. Guaranteed-character entries use the same separation rule. The final weighted catalogue reports actual multi-reel expansion probabilities; raw drawing weights alone are not the probability of paid play.

## Guaranteed marked coin boxes

When the right feature activates, every marked position reveals a value coin, collector, local multiplier or global multiplier; none is empty. Every collector repeat preserves the newly retained collector and gives every other vacancy another guaranteed reveal. All new coins finish revealing before modifiers run; new collectors then absorb the completed monetary amounts and older collectors. A retained collector alone stays dormant. Terminal value coins and the last retained collector settle exactly once.

To control the value of guaranteed coverage, raw value denominations are 0.2 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 100 / 500 base bets. Raw collector/local/global probabilities are 1.2% / 3.5% / 0.4%; the remaining 94.9% reveals a value. Base and bonus denomination weights differ and are recorded in `CONFIG`; final expected returns still depend on the complete calibrated outcome catalogue.

## Base play and the 3× XBet

Base play has a **1/200** natural bonus-entry probability. The 3× XBet has **1/40**, exactly five times the probability for the whole bonus entry event. This is not a fivefold per-scatter draw rate. Both modes use precisely the same two conditional pools: ordinary rounds and natural bonus rounds. Natural entries contain three, four, five or six visible scatters in random distinct reels and rows, and start their corresponding bonus. Every scatter tier has nonzero weight.

The natural-bonus pool was expanded to 8,192 complete entries: 7,900 start with three scatters, 240 with four, 40 with five and 12 with six. These are catalogue counts, not the final weighted probabilities. The proof reports the exact weighted tier fractions at each stake. The larger ensemble prevents the changed line rules from concentrating the conditional bonus expectation in a handful of repeated high outcomes; no payout floor or extra award is used.

At the default €0.20 stake, the weighted natural-entry tier distribution is approximately 95.797% three scatters, 2.483% four, 1.330% five and 0.390% six, conditional on a bonus entry. All four tiers have positive probability at every supported stake. These are this prototype's published weights, not private Le Zeus or other commercial game probabilities.

Let `A` be the expected ordinary-round award and `B` the expected full natural-bonus round award, in base-bet multiples. Exact pool calibration gives:

- `A = 193/400 = 0.4825`
- `B = 38793/400 = 96.9825`

Base return is `(199 A + B)/200 = 193/200 = 96.5%`.

XBet costs three bets, so its return is `(39 A + B)/(40 × 3) = 193/200 = 96.5%`.

The XBet raises entry probability while preserving the conditional game and bonus distributions. It does not secretly strengthen symbols, alter a bonus after entry or change the cap.

## Feature entries, purchased bonuses and God

The three guaranteed-character entries, four purchased bonuses and God each have their own weighted full-round pool. For each mode and stake, the proof verifies:

`sum(weight × payoutCents) × 200 = sum(weight) × costCents × 193`.

Purchased-bonus prices remain 95× / 150× / 1,800× / 2,500×. Feature entries cost 8.5× / 2.7× / 25×; these are separate from the 3× bonus-chance XBet. God costs 3,000× and its one actual MAX symbol awards the 19,999× cap only when a recorded shot hits it. God misses settle the same board. Its weighted shooting outcomes supersede the earlier procedural `4.32/30` hit-probability formula; its exact expected return also equals 96.5% against the 3,000× debit.

## Changing and checking the model

Source hashes pin the catalogue to its payline chart, procedural engine, types, model schema and builder. Development startup and production builds run a guard that rejects stale sources, inconsistent model/proof hashes, missing modes, invalid stake payouts or failed rational expectations. The selected runtime outcome is also regenerated and compared with its verified payout before any debit.

After intentionally changing mathematical rules:

```sh
npm run math:build
npm run math:check
npm run math:check:full
npm test
```

The full check independently regenerates all 425,984 stake outcomes and verifies longest payline paths and brackets, Wild substitution and summed multipliers, overlap removal, full-reel expansion, ordered follow-up shots, doubled whole-reel bonus locks and their next-spin persistence, guaranteed marked coin coverage, payouts, cap, trigger branch and all mechanic ledgers. [The simulation](mathematics-sample.json) additionally exercises real production crypto selections and reports observed variation; its observed percentage is not substituted for the exact model proof.

The completed replay checked 2,149,304 line awards, 262,888 expansions, 150,920 follow-up shots, 23,272 expanded-reel lock hits and 125,888 guaranteed coin waves, with zero ledger, path, expansion, shot, lock or reveal failures. The separate 200,000-round sample also recorded zero accounting, cap, safety or entitlement failures. Its finite observed returns ranged from 85.922% to 109.004% across modes; those observations illustrate variance and do not replace the exact 96.5% expectation.
