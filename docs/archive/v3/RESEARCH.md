# Official-source research for Studentski Grad version 3

The rewrite follows **Duck Hunters' 6×5 scatter-pay and cascade model**, while keeping original Sofia student characters, scenes, sound and Bulgarian/English controls. Version3 adds the requested party-art treatment, larger integrated stage, upgrade wheel and explicit Extra Spin dialog. It deliberately changes a normal badge from the reference's source-only boost to **source plus one other matching regular symbol**; upgraded infection continues to affect all current matches. [Duck Hunters reference and redesign](DUCK-HUNTERS-REDESIGN.md) supplies the detailed current rules, timestamped formula observations and verified public initialization. [The public paytable receipt](duck-hunters-public-paytable.json) records the nine paying symbols, all 27 bracket values and current feature prices.

The six-game comparison began on 6 October 2026 using official game sheets, title-specific feature descriptions and decoded official feature footage. Duck Hunters' public guest data was independently captured on 7 October. Three current official pages link sheets: Mental, Tombstone R.I.P and Folsom Prison. Duck Hunters, Fire in the Hole 3 and Outsourced 2 did not expose sheet PDFs; their primary pages, rules and footage supply the evidence instead.

Starting-height products describe combinations, not overall return or guaranteed wins. Each title assigns its own meaning to xWays, xSplit and xNudge; a shared mechanic name is insufficient evidence for identical mathematics. Mental's current long-form summary incorrectly discusses Das xBoot, and Duck Hunters' summary incorrectly says 5×5 and calls Bomb progress “symbol size.” Those summaries are excluded where primary sheets, rules and visible calculations disagree.

Decoded Duck Hunters footage contains digital silence despite AAC tracks. Sampled Mental audio has amplitude changes around its transition/count-up; sampled Folsom Chair sections remain relatively louder and flatter. This establishes encoded amplitude only, not melody, instrumentation or individual live-game sound effects. The game therefore uses original Web Audio cues and scene-dependent rhythm layers, with mute/volume controls and sound starting after interaction.

BG/EN controls, rules, confirmations, wheel grants, Extra Spin decisions, canvas messages and euro accounting follow the actual engine. Original curved SVG geometry provides dorm blocks, doner storefronts, parties, nightlife, an 8 December stage and expressive adult student caricatures. A larger board and integrated illustrated controls replace the earlier detached panels. Publisher sprites, photographs, footage frames and recorded sound are not shipped as production art. [Presentation](PRESENTATION.md) describes the implemented sequence.

Published commercial RTP, hit and maximum frequencies describe the publisher's own strips and definitions. They are not automatically inherited by using its public table. [Mathematics](MATHEMATICS.md) discloses this implementation's original distributions, continuation quote and measured uncertainty. The older mixed-game proposal is preserved in `archive/v1`; the preceding source-only version and its measured simulation are preserved in `archive/v2`. Those reports do not establish returns for version3's additional normal-badge target. Fresh measured results must match the revised engine hashes.
