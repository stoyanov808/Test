# Official gameplay presentation study

Research observed on 6 October 2026, before game implementation. The sources below are direct feature footage linked by the official Nolimit City game pages. FFmpeg decoded the actual videos and generated contact sheets, which were visually inspected. Contact sheets and the measured audio data are retained in `research/evidence/` for review; these are research artifacts, not game assets. Times below are approximate positions in those clips, not timing guarantees for ordinary play. These are edited promotional feature demonstrations; cuts, title cards and device showcases limit what can be inferred about uninterrupted play.

## Sound: measured evidence and limits

This model cannot listen to audio input. Therefore no claim is made about hearing melodies, instruments, voices or individual sound effects. Audio tracks were decoded and measured using FFmpeg `volumedetect`, a 180 Hz low-pass filter, and `astats`. The table reports encoded clip amplitude in dBFS, not perceived loudness or live-game mixing. A 180 Hz low-pass measurement is a simple reproducible low-frequency indicator, not a source-separated bass track.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Mental Freespins / red-door transition | 7–9 s | −24.7 / −6.8 | −25.6 |
| Mental Freespins / modifier resolution | 14–18 s | −22.8 / −8.4 | −24.1 |

| Folsom The Chair / count-up | 17–24 s | −13.5 / 0.0 | −15.2 |
| Deadwood xNudge / full clip | 0–11.44 s | All decoded samples are zero; `astats` peak and RMS are −∞ | Silent |

The Mental clip has a measurable dip around its door transition and higher amplitude during the win presentation. Folsom's edited Chair clip remains comparatively level and louder in these sampled windows. Deadwood's xNudge file includes an AAC stream but contains digital silence; this is a limitation of that clip and says nothing about the live game's soundtrack. Exact levels from unrelated encoded promotional clips should not be adopted as mixing targets.

The original Studentski Grad audio plan is street ambience plus a soft filtered nightlife pulse, then original percussion/chord layers for dorm, Friday and 8 December tiers. Reel-stop accents, note splits, Wild steps, VIP locks and win cues should be distinct, brief and generated specifically for the demo. Begin audio after interaction and provide mute/volume controls. This is an original design decision, not a claimed listening observation from Nolimit City.

## Planned pacing and visual language

Use a dominant 5×4 board and a clearly ordered visual sequence: reel deceleration/stop and bounce, Scatter count, sticky-note duplication, sequential Wild nudges, ways highlighting, current party multiplier application, payout settlement and then the energy increase for a winning bonus spin. A normal spin near 1.2 seconds with roughly 110 ms reel-stop spacing is an initial demo design target, not a measured Nolimit City rule. Turbo changes animation durations only. Big-win animations can be skipped without bypassing settlement.

Use original illustrated Studentski Grad buildings, takeaway fronts, balconies and signage around the reels. Dark asphalt, dirty ivory, concrete, amber lamps and red nightlife accents make bright paying symbols readable. Distinct pink invitations and angular gold VIP passes should stay recognizable even on a phone. Sticky-note colors, local split counts, Wild step values and a party-energy meter make the interacting mathematics understandable.

No source artwork, video frames or recorded audio are to be embedded as game assets. Local downloaded research media and contact sheets are evidence for this study, not licensed production material.

## Source quality checks

- The current Mental page contains a mismatched **Das xBoot summary** about a submarine. That summary was excluded; Mental's own header, game-sheet, named feature descriptions and actual Mental footage are the relevant sources.
- The current Duck Hunters prose summary says 5×5, but official metadata and the actual official Scatter Wins footage show 6×5. Prefer the visible gameplay grid when explaining this discrepancy.
- Embedded third-party reviews on official pages were not treated as publisher-authored observations. Edited feature clips cannot verify RNG probabilities, RTP, expected bonus duration, price stacking, general speed modes or all live transitions; those require the official game-sheet/rules or the demo's own tested engine.
