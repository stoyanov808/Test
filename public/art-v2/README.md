# Studentski Grad: original vector artwork

These SVG illustrations were authored for this game as text-based vector geometry.
They contain no photos, downloaded illustrations, textures, raster image data,
image-generation output, or Nolimit City artwork. The game does not reproduce
Duck Hunters characters, logos, environments, or asset files.

The common visual language is angular adult caricature and concrete student
district architecture, with cut contours, rough paper gaps, hard ink outlines,
and eight spot colors. The scenes leave a dark central stage to support readable
reels; their buildings, corridor, food kiosk, club frontage, and stage were drawn
directly from simple paths. Small patterns are original vector marks.

Symbol viewboxes are `220 × 240`. Scene viewboxes are `1600 × 900`.

Files: ten paying symbols; wild, scatter, and VIP; bomb, xWays, infectious,
and shot modifiers; one tall wild; four independently drawn scenes.
