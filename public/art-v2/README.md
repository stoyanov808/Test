# Studentski Grad: original illustrated artwork

Presentation 5.2 uses the [painted art set](../art-v3/README.md) for regular
symbols and scenes. This folder retains the authored feature signs and earlier
vector illustrations; its provenance describes these SVG files specifically.

The 24 SVG illustrations in this folder are original, directly authored curved
vector paths. They contain no photographs, downloaded illustrations, commercial
game assets, embedded raster data or image-generation output.

The visual language is adult editorial caricature with irregular ink contours,
selective cross-hatching, layered painted color and warm shadows. Faces are
asymmetric and expressive; clothing, student food and late-night objects have
recognizable material details. Sofia dorm blocks, a food kiosk, a lived-in dorm
kitchen, a student nightclub and a winter December 8 stage establish the setting.

Ordinary symbols use restrained material colors. The **Scatter** is a luminous
magenta party invitation with a cyan cut edge and large BONUS lettering. The
**Wild** has a gold crown/star silhouette, cream WILD banner and exuberant adult
student portrait. The infectious symbol is a standalone party speaker: its base
variant `xways.svg` has one teal speaker; `infectious.svg` and
`infectious-upgraded.svg` have two magenta speakers and gold framing. Party sound
spreads outward from the actual symbol.

All symbols use a `220 × 240` viewbox. Scenes use `1600 × 900` and the arrival
minibus uses `640 × 240`. Local gradients, shadows and sparse vector grain add
depth without external assets. All variants are static original art; gameplay
reveal, infection travel, scatter arrival and wheel timing belong to the renderer.

Nine regular paying symbols are used by the engine. `couple.svg` is a bonus
celebration illustration; `vip.svg` and `wild-tall.svg` are compatible presentation
variants. None introduces additional payable symbols or random modifiers.

The presentation revision rebuilds all **nine paying symbols**, rather than only
changing their palette. Adult portraits now have smaller heads and eyes, broader
shoulders, asymmetric facial planes and narrower jaws. Creased overshirts, jacket
lapels, phone glass, padded headphones, turntable grooves and articulated hands
establish the student-party setting at reel size. Objects have different material
construction: reflected green glass and condensation on the beer; a ceramic rim
and saucer on the coffee; irregular grilled chicken, green salad and folded paper
on the doner; bamboo grain and folded cardboard on the noodles; layered paper
edges and spiral binding on the exam notebook.

The four scenes retain their original architecture and party identity. Added
surface-aligned glazing, weathered concrete seams, kitchen tile grout, perforated
speaker grilles, uneven snow and broken wet-floor reflections add physical depth.
The Bonus and Wild silhouettes remain bright and readable beside these more
restrained paying symbols. All new contours and material marks are authored SVG
paths; no raster images, commercial sprites or downloaded art are used.

Beer projectiles and impact splashes belong to the renderer. They replay the
recorded feature events and add no symbols, paying cells or gameplay randomness.
